## 2. Composite tube fit

**Theory.** Use a frozen base $g(u,h)$, with $h$ the explicitly held inputs, and a decoder-centred lift $x_0=D(u,h)$ whose encoder agrees with $u$. For an affine chart, enforce that agreement algebraically. Choose a fixed orthonormal normal basis $N$ and write $x=x_0+Nn$ in the chosen input metric. Keep any omitted component $v=x-x_0-Nn$ separate. On feasible segments in an $r$-tube assume $\Phi$ has normal Hessian bounded by $H$, and a paired gain $L_v$ from the omitted component. Then the raw model $g(u,h)+\widehat B(u,h)n$ satisfies

$$
\|\Phi(x)-g(u,h)-\widehat B(u,h)n\|
\le E_0(u,h)+\|(D\Phi(x_0)N-\widehat B(u,h))n\|
       +\tfrac12 H\|n\|^2+L_v\|v\|.
$$

Here $E_0$ bounds the base on the *lift*, which may require independent centre labels or a small bridge from the actual plain states. Where a valid lift does not exist, refuse or use a feasible local chart. For wide maps use a few identified normal directions, score the omitted component in the bound, and stop adding directions when independent perturbations show the rank is inadequate. A constant $B$ is the first fit, then allow low-degree dependence on $(u,h)$ only if slope errors vary systematically across centres. This construction also applies to vector-valued functions directly. [Memo T2, T5, §8 and G](../MEMO.md) · [p10 normal design](../panel/generic4.py)

Fit the correction separately, using paired solver differences $\Phi(x)-\Phi(x_0)$ where feasible. They separate response to a normal offset from the base defect. With only labels $\Phi(x)-g(u,h)$, include base error at the residual training sites. For each frozen linear fit, write $S$ for its truncated-SVD operator and $\ell(t)$ for its label weights. For any comparator $q$, the triangle bound adds the point approximation error, $\|\ell\|_1$ times the largest training approximation and label error, and the reproduction term $|\phi(t)(I-V_rV_r^\mathsf T)c_q|$ for $q=\phi c_q$. The latter vanishes on the retained coefficient space. A small out-of-span fraction alone still needs a coefficient bound to control the reproduction term. Apply this calculation to the base and the centred residual separately, or to the paired derivative regression with finite-difference error included. Use a verified joint defect bound for the deployed postprocessor, or multiply a raw bound by its paired gain and add its displacement of the exact output. Verify positivity, declared laws and score-mask crossings in that same branch. [Proposition 5.1](../MEMO.md#t5-what-a-fit-from-samples-does-where-it-is-used) · [R4e projection and gate](../panel/generic4.py) · [Mask](../../src/problems/ode/step_maps.py#L52)

The additive correction is continuous through $n=0$ and vanishes there. This preserves the base exactly on the lift and requires a check on real plain states whose decoder residual is nonzero. Start with no switch between fitted models. If an inner band needs protecting, multiply the correction by a continuous weight that rises from zero to one. In that band the bound then includes the uncancelled $(1-w)D\Phi(x_0)Nn$. A blend of two independently certified models has the convex combination of their defect bounds in the same scoring coordinates. For a hard switch, certify whichever branch can be selected and any ambiguous boundary. Its output jump can be as large as the sum of the two error bounds. The exact-map error recurrence in §4 can handle a discontinuous refusal policy when its branch bounds hold and the tube closes. [Memo T2 and §8](../MEMO.md)

**Deciding experiment and predictions.** On HIRES and POLLU, freeze T0's chart, base, clipping/postprocessor and fit split; include Burgers d20 as a control. Compare base, mixed-only refit, joint normal refit and frozen-base residual at matched chart degree and identical raw label banks. Use a time-matched plain arm and charge centre, perturbed and derivative solves to the arms using them. Sample both operationally realistic split displacements and paired feasible probes at three radii along a few directions, with independent centres, neighbour histories and normal directions for the audit. At each site compare defect *changes* to the centre defect. Halving the probe radius should roughly quarter the curvature term in a direction with appreciable second derivative, once derivative estimation error is smaller than that term. Pre-register that the frozen-base residual preserves the HIRES plain maximum within 2× of base, halves weak-tube ungated maximum versus base at equal sites, and reduces the POLLU weak-mix ungated maximum below 0.005. Predict little tube improvement on Burgers. Require the strong-shell tail and untouched closed maximum to pass at useful coverage before promotion. If the first-order term persists with well-excited normals, use one $B(u,h)$ trial. If it persists mainly in unsampled directions or at invalid lifts, stop increasing polynomial degree and narrow the served tube. ## 3. A gate for the maximum

**Theory.** There are three different claims. A maximum over a labelled set describes those inputs. An independent block audit gives a failure-probability bound for a fixed policy under a specified law. A uniform accepted-input certificate requires a bound throughout a covered set. For the latter, partition a declared tube into a few cells using chart support, normal radius and held inputs. For each candidate branch and each cell certify a score $E(x)$ using §2, or cover the cell by feasible oracle sites at fill radius $h_C$ with a verified defect modulus $L_C$. Then an upper bound is $\max_i e_i+L_Ch_C+b_C$, with $b_C$ a reference error bound. Include uncertain masks conservatively on the full set of potentially scored outputs. Accept only in a supported cell when its bound fits the remaining propagated budget. Use the exact solver elsewhere and account for its own numerical error if the comparison target is the mathematical map. This is deterministic conditional on the modulus, the reference enclosure and the reachable tube. A maximum of $e/(1+b)$ on calibration samples supplies none of those assumptions. Its leverage term bounds sensitivity to label perturbations under the frozen regression, and the decoder residual measures geometry. Both can be useful ranking features. [Memo T4–T5 and §8](../MEMO.md) · [R4e calibration](../panel/generic4.py)

**Practical first gate.** Freeze the fitted model, route and risk bins before calibration on fresh whole blocks. Use a core/outer split by calibrated radius, with a small number of predeclared held-input or transient strata only where there are enough independent blocks. Reject unsupported regions, nonfinite features, bounds, decoder residuals or outputs, and invalid states. Record the rejection reason. Use leverage, normal radius and chart support as gate inputs. A step-index feature is admissible as a policy input only if the application supplies an equivalent clock; a distance-to-transient feature is a better generic test. R4e's final fit pools thresholds from different fold models. Its early all-refused shortcut also uses $b\ge1$, which follows when the constant is reproduced and the weights sum to one. TSVD requires checking that condition. Use actual decisions when the shortcut condition is unproved. Calibrate the *deployed* fit, including the pass/fail decision and physical corrections. Plain-path refusal can guide a search, while dropping a configuration on that statistic alone can lose one that would pass a closed replay. [R4e fit, gate, selection and final refit](../panel/generic4.py) · [Fold construction](../panel/generic3.py)

Calibrate or rank the candidate with training and selection blocks, then run a frozen policy on fresh independent closed blocks. The score of a block is the worst over every cell, step and scored output, with numerical, physical or solver failures counted as failures. Under exchangeability split conformal gives *marginal* coverage of the maximum of a new block, with the usual order statistic at rank $\lceil(m+1)(1-\alpha)\rceil$ and an infinity cutoff if that rank exceeds the sample size. The policy must be fixed for that score distribution. If the threshold changes the policy and hence its inputs, use a separate selection stage and a fresh final block audit, with multiplicity accounted for across repeated promotions. Under i.i.d. blocks, zero breaches in 299 fresh blocks rejects a block violation probability above 1% at 95% confidence for one frozen policy. Nearby cells and time points do not count as independent blocks. This result leaves rare misses possible. [Memo T4](../MEMO.md#t4-what-can-be-certified-from-samples) · [p11 block geometry](../panel/p11_paired_columns.py)

**Prediction.** On HIRES, the partitioned gate on the frozen-base residual should recover a nonzero safe core that the global T1 gate rejects. Require at least a 20-point improvement over T1's zero mixed acceptance under the prescribed defect budget and closed-chain tolerance to justify calibrating the outer shell. On Fisher d3 require the two sorted failures to disappear while avoiding more than half of the reference solve time. If, on the same sites, hindsight acceptance under the given defect budget cannot clear that cost threshold, change the model or regime before collecting more calibration data. If only the probabilistic audit is feasible, report the block failure bound and covered operating law explicitly. ## 4. Accumulation on POLLU

**Theory.** Write $x_{j+1}=F_j(x_j)$ for the exact *split* update, including the actual transport and drivers, and $\delta_j$ for the hybrid policy's own-input defect after applying the same split operators. Then $e_{j+1}=F_j(y_j)-F_j(x_j)+\delta_j$. On a verified tube a paired weighted-infinity gain $L_j$ gives the online recurrence $B_{j+1}=L_j B_j+d_j$, where $d_j$ bounds the selected branch and $B_0$ covers initial error. Induct on $B_j$ and tube reachability. Verified componentwise transfer bounds $|e_{j+1}|\le H_j|e_j|+d_j$ can give markedly different allowances by state component and step. For diagnosis use the signed first-order decomposition $\sum_{i<j}D F_{j-1}\cdots D F_{i+1}\delta_i$ and check the Taylor remainder against the actual signed error. Both signs and gains matter. A later exact step on the hybrid state sets that step's defect to the solver error; inherited error persists according to $F_j$. A checkpoint can reset an error budget only when replay starts from a sufficiently accurate checkpoint. Enforcing a true invariant removes error in its constrained totals and leaves errors tangent to its leaf possible. ## 6. First TT and shared-output decision

**Theory.** For a terminal latent head write $\hat f=b+D\hat a$, with fixed decoder $D$. For any comparator code $a$, the defect is bounded by the decoder residual $\|f-b-Da\|_\infty$ plus $\|D\|_{\infty\leftarrow\infty}\|a-\hat a\|_\infty$. Use scaled rows and the actual mask on a certified region, and carry postprocessing through as in §2. One can apply the linear-fit bound directly to the decoded head on frozen input features. An oracle-code audit budgets the decoder before testing any encoder. A second necessary test on a fully scored matrix of $n$ states by $m$ outputs is that a centred rank-$r$ target's SVD tail have Frobenius norm at most $\sqrt{nm}\epsilon$ if an affine rank-$r$ decoder reaches maximum error $\epsilon$. Centering is an orthogonal projection on rows. This bound holds even if the best decoder is allowed to see the whole audit matrix. Restricted always-scored components give a valid subtest with variable masks. A TT core is linear in its parameters only with the other cores fixed. Last-sweep per-core leverage cannot certify the fitted ALS map. Freeze features learned with discovery labels and fit the last linear head on separate trajectories. For independent TT, compare with reuse of vector label calls and shared basis calculations.
