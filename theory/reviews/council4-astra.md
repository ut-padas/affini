## 2. What the gate can certify

**First choose the contract.** For a deterministic certificate, let $E_{j,i}$ enclose the error in cell $i$ before step $j$, in a norm controlling all variables that affect future scored quantities. For the exact transport, require nonnegative bounds $W_{iℓ}$ on the change in transported cell $i$ from perturbing cell ℓ over the enclosure. For exact chemistry require a compatible gain $L_i$ on that transported set. If the *postprocessed* proposed state differs from the exact chemistry evaluated at the actual hybrid input by at most $d_i$, the triangle inequality gives

`E_next[i] = L[i] * sum_l(W[i,l] * E[l]) + d[i]`.

Start at zero for shared initial states. Induction bounds every intermediate state. A solver fallback contributes its numerical-error allowance relative to the chosen reference. It still propagates inherited error. In a convex linear-state stencil W can be the stencil weights. For positive abundances mixed in log coordinates, each log-output derivative with respect to donor log-abundance is its abundance-weighted fraction. A max over donor bounds is safe there, including the log floor where clipping is nonexpansive. Use tighter validated fractions only on an enclosing range. Include driver feedback for MHD. Under identity chemistry and a swap, a cell with zero accumulated local charges can receive the other cell's entire error. A “neutral” finite-time classification also supplies no one-step gain bound. A truth-dependent scoring mask can hide a species that re-enters above the floor. [Mean-Jacobian recurrence and T5](../MEMO.md) · [R4f account](../panel/generic4.py) · [closed transport](../panel/p11_paired_columns.py) · [scoring mask](../../src/problems/ode/step_maps.py)

**A two-level implementation.** Use the sketched statistics and decoder radius cheaply to select a small supported patch or refuse. Within it, anchor the defect of the *frozen deployed predictor* to independent oracle points. If a valid local Lipschitz bound $K_C$ for that defect holds along the segment to anchor $a$, then (d(x) ≤ d(a)+K_C‖x-a‖), including label uncertainty. Take the minimum over applicable anchors. A verified directional or anisotropic bound can tighten the radius. Static vector functions use the same construction with an output maximum and no propagation. For differentiable maps, an offline enclosure of the norm of the difference between the predictor Jacobian and the exact Jacobian supplies $K_C$. For an ODE this can use validated variational integration on low-dimensional patches. Charge conversion from physical to scored units, and decline patches where that enclosure is too expensive or wide. Checking distance and domain for any retrieved anchor suffices. A reduced-coordinate certificate must charge omitted directions, and a patch crossing a discontinuous switch must bound the jump or refuse. R4f's LP, fitted range caps and fixed Gaussian sketch are empirical features. The sketch has directions of underestimation, and even exact leverage controls fitted-space stability only after supplying an approximation-error bound. The shortcut assuming $b≥1$ also needs proof after truncation or sketching. Never refund charges because recent audits happened to pass. For each cell accept if support checks pass and the propagated account plus its proposed defect fits the budget. If a fallback also cannot meet the inherited budget, return to a trusted checkpoint and replay the affected dependency region including its halo, or replay the full column. Keep trial block states provisional until the block passes. Charge replay time and score every committed intermediate state. Keep lookup cheap with one shared input index and one scalar worst-component radius per patch. Precompute gains where feasible. If verified gain/defect bounds are unavailable or their cost defeats 2×, offer **empirically qualified service**. Freeze fit, strata, thresholds and fallback policy on development histories, separately from final audit columns. In that mode calibrate a few support/driver strata on labelled hybrid inputs, include the full deployed projection and monitor the whole-column maximum. The inferential unit is an independent coupled realization. For a fixed regime and policy, zero exceedances in 59 independent realizations gives the one-sided 95% binomial upper failure probability $1-0.05^{1/59}<0.05$. A run that changes thresholds consumes its audit set. Report a bound per regime, or define the mixture and the simultaneous confidence rule in advance. A finite audit cannot guarantee a maximum over every future MFC path. ## 3. Shortest route to expensive chemistry

**Primordial first.** Predict the next **chemistry increment** at the current transported input using $2Δ_{j-1}-Δ_{j-2}$, where each Δ is post-chemistry state minus its own transported input in solver coordinates. It is a vector operation with linear storage and arithmetic in species count. Begin with two exact steps. This is a generic history-conditioned candidate for time sequences. It can be added beside a memoryless predictor without assuming a low-rank network. The local defect on the *actual history* is exactly the second difference of chemistry increments if the previous increments were exact there. With errors in the two stored increments it increases by at most twice the most recent error plus the preceding error. If increments come from a twice differentiable time curve with a bounded second derivative, equally spaced exact samples bound the second difference by that derivative bound times the squared spacing. A transport or driver change can invalidate that smoothness assumption. Treat the previous differences and changes in drivers and transported states as support variables, admit only histories on which the chosen gate qualifies the current full-vector defect, and invalidate history on a cell remap without known correspondence. Check startup and every later state. ## 4. Opportunities worth at least 2× against VODE

1. **Establish a successful denominator.** P14 restarts Fortran VODE/BDF with Python RHS and Jacobian, on every test tracer at steps 0, 10, 20. Panel test counts vary. Its stored infinity tells us that at least one scored result failed the finite comparison. It does not count failures or give their return codes. I tested an equivalent change of variables with $D_{ii}=\max(y_{0,i},\mathrm{atol}/\mathrm{rtol})$, $y=Dv$, $t=Δt\,τ$. Integrate to τ=1 with RHS $Δt D^{-1}f(Dv)$, Jacobian $Δt D^{-1}J(Dv)D$, initial $D^{-1}y_0$, same relative tolerance and absolute tolerance vector $D^{-1}\mathrm{atol}$. This preserves the physical component error weights. It changes the arithmetic and initialization of the integration. Use the physical Jacobian including the system's own held-input convention.
