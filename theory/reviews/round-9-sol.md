## Round 8 findings

- **Finding 1. Not resolved — Proposition 8.2 and §10 item 3.** The triangle inequality now uses the right pairs. Insert `Ĝ(χ(π(p)))` and `Γτ(π(p))` between `Ĝ(π̂(p))` and `Φτ(p)`. A finite bound on those pairs suffices, but the display uses `Lip_Q(Ĝ)`, the Lipschitz constant over every pair in `Q`, which the hypothesis need not make finite. The parenthetical landing condition can also be read as dispensing with Lipschitz control altogether. The circle calculation gives `|(1+A)h|` (take `A,h>0`), but the identity on the whole circle is an ambient embedding. It is not a single chart of the one-dimensional manifold. Define a finite paired constant `L` and use it in the display. When the learned code stays in the exact code image, Lipschitz control there supplies `L`. Allow an embedding explicitly or give a strict local-chart example. For example, with zero rotation, use the angle chart and take the inputs selected for that chart to have exact code image `[-a,0]` within the open chart. Shift the learned code by `h>0`, and extend the exact circle decoder by adding `A max(u,0)e₁` to `(cos u,sin u)`. At angle zero the first-coordinate error is `|cos h − 1 + Ah|`, while the Lipschitz constant on the exact image is at most 1. For an atlas, bound every chart the implementation can select and take the maximum of the resulting bounds.

- **Finding 2. Not resolved — Corollaries 6.3–6.4.** The new three-term estimate and its decomposition are correct with a finite paired Lipschitz constant for `Γτ`. The counterexample checks out. - **Finding 3. Resolved — Demonstration 8.5 and the rollout rule.** The first-order sum and Jacobian products are called diagnostics, distinct from the measured chained error. With `ε,C>0`, the example visits `0, ε, 2ε+C`. Since `h(0)=h′(0)=h′(1)=0` and `h(1)=1`, the first-order sum on the exact chain is `2ε` and the Jacobian products agree on both chains. Any tolerance strictly between `2ε` and `2ε+C` makes the joint diagnostic pass and the rollout fail. State those parameter choices explicitly. In the sufficiency sentence, give Taylor's remainder bound and tube closure as one route, and an exact mean-Jacobian recurrence with tube closure as another. Proposition 2.2 as printed gives the Taylor representation.

- **Finding 5. Not resolved — Theorem 8 scope and the deployed map (§8, §G, §10 item 1).** The memo now separates the empirical HIRES map from the factored theorem, correctly. ## New problems in the revision

1. **Major — T3, Corollary 3.2 and §10 item 5.** The displayed bound is correct, but uniform `ν` and `ℓΔτ` suffice only if the time horizon is fixed or the exponential factor is otherwise controlled. Take `x₁′=x₁+x₂/d` with all other components stationary and `τ=d`. Then `ν=1`, `ℓΔτ=1` and unit half-widths give a radius-zero error of `(exp(d)−1)/d`. The new §10 summary mentions only `ℓΔτ` and misses even the fixed-time requirement on `ν`. State uniform bounds on `h*`, `exp(ντ)` and `ℓΔτ` in both locations.
