## 2. Which changes could cut refusal several-fold?

**What can be certified.** Use the user's norm, for example a maximum of component errors divided by application-supplied positive tolerances, and retain every component that can affect a future scored output in the propagation analysis. Let transport's nonnegative gains on the reachable enclosure be Wᵢⱼ, the exact map's compatible gain Lᵢ, and a verified postprocessing-inclusive local defect bound at the actual hybrid input be dᵢ. Then the triangle inequality gives E′ᵢ ≤ Lᵢ Σⱼ Wᵢⱼ Eⱼ + dᵢ. With identical starts, induction bounds every step, including splits. For a refusal use the exact integrator's error allowance relative to the reference. An exact step at a perturbed input propagates inherited error. For a static scalar or vector function only dᵢ is needed. This is a conditional theorem. A generic black box need supply neither the gains nor a regularity bound. An unseen narrow bump can agree at every labelled point and arbitrarily change the error between them. [Derivation and feedback-state caveat](council4-astra.md) · [actual account and projection](../panel/generic4.py)

R4g's LP constrains finite sampled residuals up to LP feasibility tolerance. CHEMAKZO/340 actually stores four zero envelope coefficients and 100% of test trajectories above the envelope. The account sums a typical NNLS charge in fixed cell order, charges zero on refusals and tests λ(S+c). It neither transports S nor multiplies inherited error by a local gain. λ is tuned using the same trajectories used to fit the gate, pooling three regimes with shared starts and exact donors. The observed ≤5% pooling criterion gives no stated confidence level for a new closed column. Even the cap's training-set argument needs qualification. With an exact truncated SVD the average squared leverage equals r. Under the deployed Gaussian sketch it equals the squared Frobenius norm of its r-by-32 sketch matrix, whose **expectation** is r. The deterministic Markov fraction for that fit is its realized mean divided by 9r. Neither argument concerns a transported input. **A usable distributional contract.** Freeze the entire policy including precision, routing, restart and fallback. Draw independent whole realizations of the declared deployment law and horizon. A failure is *any* scored cell/component/step over tolerance. With zero failures in n independent realizations, the one-sided 95% binomial upper bound is 1−0.05^(1/n), from (1−p)^n=0.05. Thus 59 clean realizations bound p below 5% for that law. For multiple promised regimes test each with a prespecified confidence allocation, or score the maximum across all regimes in each independent replicate. Use a new audit after any tuning. ## 3. GPU inference and batching

**Where a GPU can change the decision.** Price a whole service interval as common work C, surrogate/gate work H(B) and time F for the actual compacted fallback queues, versus C+V for all-exact. For serial phases, 2× requires H+F ≤ (V−C)/2. If phases overlap, measure the critical path directly. Under uniform additive solve prices and negligible common work, F/V=ρ and speed ≤1/ρ. On a scalar replay with heterogeneous costs use the fraction of exact *time* spent on refused states. On GPU, F depends on queue lengths, stiffness and divergence. A mean cell fraction cannot determine it. In P17, subtracting ρ from inverse estimated speed gives a median H/V of 0.482 on Fisher d10, at only 3.3–3.9% refusal. Halving ρ alone leaves nearly half of exact time in evaluation. On CHEMAKZO/340, 97–100% refusal rules out a 2× win at uniform prices even with free evaluation. These cases need different interventions. [P17 per-column components](../panel/data/p17_audit.json) · [P15 single-state denominators](../panel/data/p15_denominators.json)

**Batch workload.** With p terms, o predicted components and two 32-column sketches, the shared design row times the three coefficient blocks costs about 2p(o+64) floating operations per cell, besides chart, basis, norms, decoder, projection and masks. At p=715 and o=20 this is 120,120 operations per cell and about 469 KiB of reusable FP64 weights. A materialized design adds 5,720 bytes per cell, about 22.3 MiB for 4,096 cells. Account.refuses currently computes the design and both sketch products again. Share the statistics within one evaluation, keep the state and account on device, then compact refused indices for an exact solve and scatter once. Benchmark a CPU implementation with the same reuse. If every cell is refused by a cheap sound pretest, bypass the unused prediction. At full retained rank the mathematical complement is zero. Treat removing the numerical complement test as a policy change requiring a new audit; POLLU/340 has rank 560 of 560. ## 4. Which training stages belong on a GPU?

1. **Move repeated dense work first.** Given cached inputs and exact successors, the chart, design, truncated SVD and multi-output solve are dense operations. Batch whole calibration scenarios, keeping trajectories and policy state resident through each sequence. Fit4 explicitly builds U as well as A, and final rebuilds the chosen degree's fold fits for pooled calibration before calibrate_fresh replaces the resulting gate. Cache fold statistics or bypass superseded work only after comparing the selected degree, final fit, gate, masks and chain maxima. Leave the four-coefficient LP and NNLS on CPU initially, transfer compact calibration features, and profile. R4g/340 train_s (including selection and calibration) is 14.7 s for Fisher d10 and 664 s for HIRES. These totals do not attribute bottlenecks. Record device-hours, exact-label seconds, peak workspace and time per stage before accelerating SVD. 2. **Oracle generation sets its own placement.** P.step currently calls a CPU integrator per row. A generic callable can be host-only. Expose a batched label interface accepting either host or device implementations. For a GPU-capable oracle, group independent trajectories and compact adaptive exact work as in inference. Keep causal steps sequential. Otherwise generate labels on CPU workers, cache them once, and stage chunks to GPU. Run the same projected/held state and tighter-reference tests for any new exact implementation. Given the offline budget, prioritize the model with the best deployed maximum, fallback time and throughput. Report break-even number of service intervals as offline cost divided by positive time saved per interval on a common cost basis. Use stable logical blocks for reductions. Fix singular-vector sign convention and handle near-degenerate subspaces: a basis rotation leaves an exact subspace projector invariant but can change a fixed coordinate-space random sketch, or even the chart features. Save the deployed chart, coefficients and sketch matrices. Selection also measures time to choose among admissible degrees within 10% of cheapest, so deterministic linear algebra alone cannot ensure the same chosen model. Separate reproducible candidate results from a frozen hardware cost table/tie rule used to select. Repeat trainings, permute physical batch/rank allocations, compare rank, degree, masks, charges, maxima and worst witnesses. Preserve CPU bits for refactors and deterministic per-segment GPU operations and exact leaf fits in the library. They do not implement a global truncated SVD. ## 5. Several GPUs

**Distribute independent work before splitting a fit.** Assign whole problems, folds, seed draws and independent closed realizations to workers with immutable input IDs and atomic case records. Degrees have an early stopping rule, λ trials stop on the first admissible value, and coupled cells in one column exchange neighbours. Parallelize trials only where results can be reordered into the prescribed decisions and where speculative work is affordable. Independent exact-label batches can be dynamically scheduled while preserving row and RNG identity. Time one, two and four GPUs at fixed total work and at fixed work per GPU. Record the slowest worker, makespan, idle fraction, bytes crossing each link, and device-hours. Predict better efficiency for whole-fold or realization scheduling than for distributing one modest panel fit. A serial oracle, I/O or one long candidate can cap either scheme. [select and calibrate_fresh](../panel/generic4.py) · [P17 closed stencil](../panel/p17_audit.py) · [existing work sharing](../../src/engine/work_stealing.py) · [case-store rules](../../AGENTS.md)

**A large global fit needs a stable distributed solve.** Fix the same global centering, chart, feature ranges and basis before row-sharding A and Y. Normal equations communicate ΣAᵢᵀAᵢ and ΣAᵢᵀYᵢ, O(p²+po) numbers per collective, with no iterative gradient synchronization. But κ(AᵀA)=κ(A)². At Fit4's relative singular cutoff 10⁻¹⁰, the corresponding relative eigenvalue is 10⁻²⁰, below FP64 unit roundoff. A Gram eigensolve can alter rank, pseudoinverse and leverage even at identical training residual. Use Gram only for demonstrably well-conditioned candidates with direct-SVD checks around the cutoff. For the general case, QR-factor each fixed row block, keep Rᵢ and QᵢᵀYᵢ, QR stacked R blocks in a fixed tree while applying the same transformations to the transformed targets. SVD the final R, truncate at the original cutoff, solve for coefficients and construct the two gate sketches once. Orthogonality preserves the singular values in exact arithmetic. Upper levels communicate O(p²+po) numbers per tree edge and perform O(p³) work per merge. Use a logical reduction tree independent of GPU count when demanding reproducibility across worker counts. Predict rank-sensitive disagreements for Gram on near-threshold synthetic spectra, then test recorded rank-deficient fits and masks. A polynomial basis that outgrows local memory or the row count per worker defeats strong scaling. [Fit4 and term limits](../panel/generic4.py) · [rank 632/715 example](../panel/data/records/r4g/bruss_d20_340.json)

**Inference can replicate the compact model.** Give each GPU its cells, persistent account and local fallback queue. Independent function calls require no inter-device communication after replication. For a coupled map each transport step still exchanges halos and any feedback fields. A proof using neighbour error budgets also needs neighbouring bounds. Keep ownership aligned with the transport decomposition and preserve stable cell IDs during packing, migration and retries. Rebalance only if measured fallback imbalance exceeds migration/synchronization cost. Compare against the same multi-GPU allocation running exact solves. A neural DDP implementation trades gradient collectives over repeated training iterations for flexible parameterization. Here the fit needs a few algebraic reductions or independent candidate jobs, and both families can replicate their predictors at inference. Compare at equal maximum-error target and cost of fallback on identical realizations.
