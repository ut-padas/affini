## 2. Composite tube model

**Bound first.** Freeze a chart and a section through its fibres. In local coordinates write the input as J(u) + n with u including held drivers, and n the offset from the decoded centre in scaled state coordinates. Require the section to map back to u and the segment to stay feasible. With floors or constraints, work in a smooth admissible patch or refuse the crossing. The P10 discarded PCA coordinates are offsets from a *linear plane* and can be nonzero on a curved centreline. Subtract the decoded normal coordinate at u. If a unique section does not describe the data, use multiple local patches or retain another coordinate. [P10 features](../panel/generic4.py) · [decoder](../panel/generic2.py) · [reduced-coordinate assumptions](../MEMO.md)

Let g(u) approximate the exact centre step with uniform error ε₀. Freeze it. Fit an additive head B(u)n, initially a constant matrix, using feasible, paired offsets. Bounded transverse Hessian H and derivative mismatch at most η in compatible operator norm give
**‖Φ(J(u)+n) − g(u) − B(u)n‖ ≤ ε₀ + η‖n‖ + H‖n‖²/2** by Taylor on the segment. The centre is unchanged at n = 0. If fitted slopes omit directions, charge their derivative times the omitted offset. For a noisy/estimated section charge its position error times an appropriate Lipschitz constant. Prefer differences of two exact successors at the same chart coordinate to prevent centre error leaking into slope labels; count both solves, and record any tangential mismatch. Use feasible directional secants where a decoder centre itself is inadmissible. A few shared normal inputs and a matrix mapping them to *all* outputs avoids fitting a separate chart per component. [T5 and §8](../MEMO.md) · [joint-fit comparator](../panel/p10_tube.py)

A continuous ramp w(r) from zero to one over a frozen overlap is justified only if the centre-only arm is better across a finite inner band. For the additive design, omitting (1−w)B n adds at most (1−w)‖B n‖ to the bound above. Alternatively convex blending bounds point error by the corresponding weighted sum of valid bounds on the two pieces. A narrow overlap adds a slope proportional to Lip(w) times the disagreement, relevant to propagation. A hard switch can jump by that disagreement. For either fixed linear head, T5 bounds fitting error in its *retained* span by (1+Λ) times its best uniform approximation error, plus Λ times label error. Paired labels can carry twice the individual label uncertainty. Add the centre and correction bounds when adding estimates; avoid double counting Taylor and fitted residual errors. Check the final clipping/projection separately or include its Lipschitz and consistency errors. [Proposition 5.1](../MEMO.md) · [deployed evaluation](../panel/generic4.py)

**Deciding experiment.** Freeze T0 chart, scaling, centre degree, laws and held-out trajectories on HIRES and POLLU. Compare frozen centre, joint polynomial with the same normal columns, frozen centre plus residual, and that residual with one prespecified ramp. Include a centre-only arm with equal-cost plain labels sampled at the same time indices, a no-mixed-label slope fit, and CHEMAKZO at matched degree as a rotation control. Use positive feasible offsets at several radii, both signs where possible, and independent split directions. Score the worst error by actual offset, centre preservation, accepted own defect, column maximum, activation and fallback. Preregister that on HIRES the anchored fit reproduces T0 on the section to rounding and halves the weak-mix ungated maximum versus T0 at matched rows; on POLLU it improves weak-mix error but alone misses a rollout. These predictions can fail independently. Require ≤0.01 on every held-back chain and ≥2× actual speed before serving. If slope helps only the median, stop at the last safe radius; if matched full-rank CHEMAKZO rotation helps substantially, inspect rank, floor and gate decisions before crediting a basis change. ## 3. What an honest gate can say

**Bound first.** For fixed truncated design A = UᵣSᵣVᵣᵀ plus discarded modes, the deployed *raw linear head* uses weights ℓ = φVᵣSᵣ⁻¹Uᵣᵀ. Its b = √N‖φVᵣSᵣ⁻¹‖₂ upper-bounds ‖ℓ‖₁. The orthogonal remainder s measures how much of the feature vector lies outside the retained right subspace. With no bound on coefficients of discarded functions, even tiny s need not bound their output. With a proved uniform approximation error and bounded solver error, T5 yields a pointwise bound in the reproduced space. The maximum observed e/(1+b) estimates a scale; it does not upper-bound best approximation on unseen inputs. A deterministic maximum additionally needs regularity and coverage, e.g. a valid defect Lipschitz constant L and a verified radius h from audited samples imply sample maximum + Lh. Propagate that bound in a norm controlling all feedback coordinates. [T5, T4 and §E](../MEMO.md) · [Fit4, calibrate and final](../panel/generic4.py)

**Policy and independent audit.** Freeze the final model first. Use development histories to set at most three strata (centre, supported transverse band, unsupported) and separate scales by radius; include time or driver phase only if observable in deployment and useful on independent histories. Refuse unsupported directions, nonfinite outputs and empty strata. Calibrate the postprocessed output, including the floor and invariant projection. On separate development columns pick the cheapest policy passing the whole-column margin; then freeze *all* thresholds. Score another exchangeable sample of whole coupled realizations by the largest error anywhere in each, including fallback. A split-conformal upper order statistic of these scores, rank ceil((m+1)(1−α)), covers a new such maximum marginally at 1−α (infinite if rank exceeds m). For a fixed policy, zero failed independent columns out of m give the one-sided 95% upper failure rate 1−0.05^(1/m); 59 suffice for under 5%. Forty cells in one column count as one column. This audit still measures an empirical maximum on each realized path and cannot guarantee all future paths or an MFC distribution shift. Changing acceptance changes the path and requires fresh audit data. [T4 and §E](../MEMO.md) · [column design](../panel/p11_paired_columns.py)

Register that a centre stratum accepts some accurate HIRES states even if the wide band is sent to the solver; require more than half of *baseline solve time* safely avoided, including gate cost for a 2× pass. Compare against global calibration at the same final chain tolerance and checkpoint budget. Report failures and exact solver cost by stratum; never count zero defects on empty acceptance as success. Explicitly re-evaluate full-rank out-of-span decisions with the mathematical s = 0 control. CHEMAKZO's s_max is 3.53e−15; a full-rank gate comparing roundoff to a sample maximum can change decisions without identifying unsupported directions. ## 4. Accumulation on POLLU

**Bound first.** Write Fⱼ for the full exact split step and dⱼ for the signed deployed defect evaluated on the hybrid input, with dⱼ = 0 on an exact fallback (relative to that solver). The fundamental theorem of calculus gives eⱼ₊₁ = Kⱼeⱼ + dⱼ, where Kⱼ is the derivative of Fⱼ averaged over the segment joining exact and hybrid inputs. Thus the final error is a sum of transported defect vectors. Uniform bounds on these products over an enclosing tube give a deterministic budget. Products along just the reference chain are a diagnostic. Projection removes components forbidden by *valid* invariants but leaves errors tangent to their level sets. An occasional exact step propagates existing error. An actual reset needs a trustworthy anchor, for example replay from the last independently accurate checkpoint. For a block surrogate an analogous bound uses its maximum block defect and the exact block's gain; check **every interior step** too. ## 6. One TT and shared-output decision

**Bound first.** A terminal output core gives a vector predictor b(x) + h(x)W. A TT may compress evaluation of the shared input features h. Freeze all feature cores and fit W by truncated SVD on an independent head design. For each output T5 then bounds the retained-space approximation error times 1+Λ, plus label noise times Λ. The final ALS update by itself supplies no bound for internal core selection or for unrepresented functions. If a small output subspace U is imposed on the increments, its own uniform reconstruction error ε_dec consumes budget, and coordinate error ε_a contributes at most ‖U‖∞→∞ ε_a. An oracle reconstruction of held-out increments in that *fixed* subspace bounds from below the error attainable there when coefficients are chosen optimally in the scoring norm. Ordinary PCA reconstruction only tests one choice of coefficients. A shared-feature head can predict every output without a separate fitted input model per output. ## 8. KIDA route

A global 0.01-dex fit is an unestablished possibility. It is a poor next expenditure. P8 local coverage grows 1.77%, 5.83%, 13.65%, 17.40% on one nested permutation of independent dark training tracers scored on the same 40 test histories. At 160 its worst is 1.99 dex and only 6.875% are within 0.005 dex. P5 gives 5.78% within 0.01 on once-mixed states. The first two doublings exceed 2× coverage, and the chart's 40-to-160 increase is 3.44 percentage points; both contradict parts of P8's prediction. The last-two-point exponent 0.350 yields 3,259 tracers for **half** the states on a literal extrapolation. It has no interval, no worst-error implication and no independent replication. In addition, Data builds the PCA chart on all 160 fitted tracers even for the 20-tracer arm. P8 measures label count conditional on that available input geometry. P9 confirms stored/dense key equality and dense/tighter agreement within 1.80e−6 dex at three sampled steps on dark histories, with the same network and BDF family. It does not validate the astrophysics, every driver, or the closed-loop trajectory.
