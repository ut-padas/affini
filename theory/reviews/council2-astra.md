## 2. A versatile TT, ranked

**Bound first.** Freeze a feature vector h(x), including its input chart and any TT contractions. Fit an unregularized full-column-rank multi-output head on design H. At x its weights are ℓ(x) = h(x)ᵀH⁺ and Λ = ‖ℓ‖₁. For each output the prediction error is at most (1 + Λ) times the best uniform error in span(h) on the head samples **and x**, plus Λ times a uniform label-error bound. This follows by subtracting any reproduced function. It applies algebraically even to a realized span learned elsewhere. Use disjoint representation, head and audit data to assess transfer. The gate can upper-bound sampling amplification. To bound the defect it also needs approximation and label-error bounds. Neither a TT-cross pivot inverse nor the sensitivity of the final ALS microproblem bounds the whole data-dependent solver. A final linear refit has a meaningful guarantee in its frozen space only. ## 3. Which output structure can be shared?

For a baseline b(x) known at evaluation (zero, persistence or a cheap physical step), write f(x) − b(x) = μ + U a(x) + r(x). In fixed scaled output units, if ‖r‖∞ ≤ η over the domain and the head approximates coordinates with maximum error ε, the vector error is at most **η + ‖U‖∞→∞ ε**. Deduct both amounts from the defect budget. With exact linear dependencies use their exact reconstruction first, in the coordinates where they hold. For approximate dependencies estimate the tail on held-out states and include threshold crossings. A known input baseline changes the appropriate rank, and even exact conservation in abundance coordinates need not be a linear relation between log increments. A frozen shared feature head can also regress all outputs directly, retaining the componentwise T5 statement without assuming any low output rank. ## 5. A tube that can support a bound

**Define membership before choosing a basis.** Let admissible base points be B(u), u in a supported chart patch including driver and step-size coordinates where variable. A query x supplies a witness u with ‖x − B(u)‖ ≤ r(u), physical constraints satisfied, and u inside the *covered* parameter set. This certifies membership of the defined tube when these checks use the actual state in the stated norm and a verified base map. A learned decoder with small residual at a code in an empty gap certifies only distance to its own image. Include lift uncertainty and floor/clipping ambiguity, or refuse. Overlapping patches need a valid bound for every selectable patch. The user supplies near-bundle use as a design premise. Measure the actual radii and boundary visits on held-back MFC input histories. [Memo §8](../MEMO.md) · [current decoder residual](../panel/generic2.py) · [target regime](../SYNTHESIS.md)

**Three possible defect bounds, with different data requirements.**

- For a centre-only head, let the verified base error be e₀(u) and assume a paired normal gain ‖Φ(x)−Φ(B(u))‖ ≤ L⊥‖x−B(u)‖. Then the defect is ≤ e₀(u)+L⊥r(u). For a fitted normal derivative B₁ and a Hessian bound H along admissible normal segments the extra terms are ≤ ‖B₁−D⊥Φ‖r + Hr²/2, plus any ignored-direction allowance. Under (S), Prop. 8.2 instead adds the paired *head* Lipschitz constant times **encoder error** and C dist(x,S) exp(−γτ) to the base error. That paired constant does not simply multiply geometric radius unless code error is bounded by radius. [Memo §8, Prop. 8.2 and gradient tier](../MEMO.md)
- For a direct chart/TT-feature fit in coordinates (u,v) spanning feasible tangent and active normal variation, use the frozen-head inequality of answer 2 on the occupied joint design. Add a bound on directions left out. Design along physical arcs with nested Chebyshev points where the map is smooth and the interval feasible. Across the tube use symmetric feasible offsets and actual split displacements. On irregular patches thin a feasible pool by leverage with explicit boundary, driver and phase strata. The tensor-product Lebesgue bound needs the actual tensor grid and a reproducing interpolant. It does not transfer to scattered or TT-cross points by giving them Chebyshev coordinates. [Memo T5](../MEMO.md) · [R6 implementation](../panel/r6_design.py)
- A simpler **local certificate** uses vector labels at surrounding physical nodes. For nonnegative weights summing to one, query-to-barycentre mismatch d₀, reference uncertainty b, and bounded first and second derivatives L,H on the containing convex neighbourhood, Taylor expansion at the query gives error ≤ b + L d₀ + (H/2)Σᵢwᵢ‖xᵢ−x‖². The sampling factor is one. Use this as a low-dimensional patch control where neighbours surround the query. With signed weights use absolute weights in the remainder/noise terms. Without verified derivative bounds this is a risk score for audit. It avoids trying to triangulate 578 coordinates. [Derivation from affine reproduction in memo T5 and covering argument in T4](../MEMO.md)

## 8. Conservation laws and the reach of the bug

**Independent falsification.** I loaded the cached training trajectories for each problem, reproduced `invariants_of`, and evaluated the actual RHS after independently recombining component values from its test pool in native coordinates. The defining identity W F(y)=0 must hold also at such positive chemistry states or admissible lattice fields for a *global* law. A finite counterexample suffices. Among 64 probes, max |F Wᵀ| with each RHS normalized to unit length was 0.771 on Brusselator d20, **0.849 on Burgers d10, 0.660 on Burgers d20, and 0.767 on POLLU**. The latter three returned 2, 2 and 6 putative laws. An independent 512-row probe on POLLU found three singular values of the residual matrix exceeding 0.003. On Burgers mass is exact by the telescoping periodic stencil. Near-constancy along a particular trajectory family can coexist with a false extra constraint. Even a genuine invariant of a restricted family requires a proof that the split update stays within that family. for name in ("bruss_d20", "burgers_d10", "burgers_d20", "pollu"):

## Ordered experiments and stopping decisions

These are proposed one-core CPU caps, excluding implementation. Freeze splits, thresholds, horizons, forcing, timed batches and predictions before running. Selection gets a 0.005 chain margin, final evaluation requires ≤0.01 maximum at every specified exchange and on ordinary rollouts, with ≥2× actual hybrid speed in each intended service regime. Record the accepted own-input maximum and test its chosen local defect budget separately. Keep the strict all-species KIDA score beside the 13-species score.
