## 2. A gate that serves and certifies

1. **Make local defect and propagation independent obligations.** My plain Burgers replay finds 31 accepted inputs with own-input error above the envelope by more than 1e-12. The worst rollout state is at step 2, tracer 27, component 14 (zero-based tracer and component). Its defect is +0.010386, its inherited contribution is zero to the reported precision, and the gate bound and account are both 0.003265. Fisher d3 also underbounds a defect at the first step, with a peak of 0.005379 against 0.003219. Better propagation alone cannot cure either. Conversely, an exact step on the hybrid input preserves any inherited error that the dynamics transmit. The sampled Euclidean unsplit amplification in the benchmark does not establish a split infinity-norm gain. [Read-only replay](#read-only-checks) · [Account and test](../panel/generic4.py#L450) · [Benchmark dynamics](../../src/benchmark/step_maps.py#L481) · [Memo T2](../MEMO.md#t2-how-a-surrogates-defect-accumulates)

2. **Use a local bound on the deployed residual, with two levels of cost.** For a frozen vector predictor `S` including projection, clipping and decoding, let `R = S − F`, with `F` the exact step or static target. On a patch, certify a Lipschitz constant `L` for `R` in the chosen input metric and full output norm. At an anchor `a`, let `d_a` be a bound on its residual including reference error. Then `d_a + L dist(x,a)` bounds the defect wherever the Lipschitz proof applies. Use a cheap precomputed patch bound for ordinary accepts, then the minimum of verified anchor bounds or a tighter local enclosure for near-budget inputs. Refuse outside proved support or if the tighter bound still costs too much or exceeds the budget. Use an input metric with explicit normal and driver widths. If applying this in a chart, bound the residual over its full physical preimage, including unresolved directions. A bound along the bundle is insufficient off it. For a rank-reduced fit one can also use Memo T5 with a *proved* approximation error in the retained space, plus output-processing terms. Exact leverage controls sampling amplification under that hypothesis. A Gaussian sketch and a fitted LP can rank or reject points, or choose which patches to certify. They do not supply the hypothesis. At 32 sketch columns even the expected squared norm identity gives no one-sided uniform guarantee, and the sampled caps are separate marginal maxima. Fail closed on invalid inputs, arithmetic or physics, and avoid the `b >= 1` early shortcut in the sketched arm unless proved for that implementation. [Memo T4–T5](../MEMO.md#t4-what-can-be-certified-from-samples) · [Sketch and caps](../panel/generic4.py#L93) · [LP and pooled final fit](../panel/generic4.py#L405)

3. **Carry inherited uncertainty through transport before deciding on chemistry.** For a closed mesh let `u_k` enclose each cell's scored-coordinate error in a fixed full-state norm, with all coupled variables represented or bounded. Bound the exact split operator on paired states in the reachable tube by a nonnegative gain matrix `A_k`, including neighbours, driver feedback, mesh operations and chemistry as applicable. The proposed step then has `u_{k+1} <= A_k u_k + d_k` componentwise, where `d_k` bounds the deployed own-input defect and includes the fallback integrator's error where used. This follows by adding and subtracting the exact split update at the hybrid state and applying the verified propagation bound. Start with a valid enclosure and induct over every substep whose states count. A moving score mask requires the full-state recurrence with a valid score conversion or a conservative active union including possible crossings and unscored species that feed back. Use a preverified schedule of caps with enough headroom that fallback remains admissible. If inherited error already exhausts the cap, replay from a checkpoint with enough certified margin or block the step before accepting it. An exact solve at the current hybrid input does not reset the account. For static functions the same local certificate is sufficient, with no propagation recurrence. [Memo T2 and T5](../MEMO.md#t2-how-a-surrogates-defect-accumulates) · [Score mask](../../src/problems/ode/step_maps.py#L52) · [Existing fallback](../../src/benchmark/step_maps.py#L459) · [Closed split operator](../panel/p11_paired_columns.py#L45)

   Fisher supplies a concrete gain check. For its positive Neumann lattice, in log state `z = log10(y)` the off-diagonal Jacobian of the transformed RHS is `L_ij y_j/y_i >= 0`. Its row sums are `−r y_i`, since diffusion ratios are invariant to a uniform log shift. Thus on a positive tube with all abundances at least `m` during the step the infinity-norm gain is at most `exp(−r m dt)`. For fixed convex physical mixing, if each donor lies within a factor `10^u` of its reference, so does their positive sum. Take the maximum donor log bound and apply the chemistry gain. The same proof does not assume a discounted gain for Burgers, HIRES or an arbitrary MFC update. Verify the positive tube and solver error when using the Fisher bound. This test distinguishes a failure of the fitted local charge from a failure of the transport argument. Select on a separate development set and run an untouched audit including difficult exchange, driver and step-duration strata. Record the signed defect and inherited terms at the same peak and audit accepted steps for underbounds. Count any violation within a block, including failed or nonphysical states under the frozen policy. A one-sided tolerance argument for a predeclared law is straightforward: zero failures in `N` independent blocks rejects a failure mass above `η` at level `(1−η)^N`. For 1% and 95% confidence a single frozen test takes 299 such blocks. Thousands of cells or times within one column do not supply those independent draws. Allocate the error probability across strata or adaptive attempts if making simultaneous claims. This is a distributional statement with no uniform guarantee from finite tests alone. Use the LP or a small stratified residual estimate for the empirical arm and use disjoint audits for qualification. The plain R4e proxy gives evaluator targets of about 11.0, 7.75 and 8.66 µs respectively for a further 2× on the first three.
